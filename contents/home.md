I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering at the [School of Electronic Information and Communications (EIC)](https://english.eic.hust.edu.cn/), [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://english.hust.edu.cn/). Previously, I received both my M.E. degree in Control Science and Engineering (2024) and B.E. degree in Automation (2021) from [China University of Geosciences (Wuhan)](https://en.cug.edu.cn/).

My current research interests center on embodied intelligence and robot control, particularly perception-action integration for expressive humanoid robots. I am interested in connecting visual perception, affective understanding, decision-making, and real-time actuation so that robots can perceive human states and respond through physically grounded behaviors. My work focuses on [facial robotics](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-95-2098-5_5), robot perception and control, and facial expression generation, while my previous research also includes [remote photoplethysmography (rPPG)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6579/ad2f5d). Through this interdisciplinary direction, I aim to develop responsive embodied intelligent systems for natural human-robot interaction.
