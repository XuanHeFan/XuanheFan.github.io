I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering at the [School of Electronic Information and Communications (EIC)](https://english.eic.hust.edu.cn/), [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://english.hust.edu.cn/). Previously, I received both my M.E. degree in Control Science and Engineering (2024) and B.E. degree in Automation (2021) from [China University of Geosciences (Wuhan)](https://en.cug.edu.cn/).

My research centers around intelligent perception and control for human-robot interaction, with a particular focus on [facial robotics](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-95-2098-5_5), [remote photoplethysmography (rPPG)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6579/ad2f5d), and facial expression generation. I am interested in connecting camera-based human sensing, interpretable facial representations, and real-time robot control to build expressive and responsive humanoid systems for natural human-robot interaction.
